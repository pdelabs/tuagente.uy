# Research 2 — Demand signal: which AI capabilities do SMBs actually buy (8/19/2026)

Research agent covering pricing pages, adoption surveys, and 2025-2026
churn data.

## Capabilities with proven demand

1. **24/7 AI receptionist with scheduling**: Rosie US$49/month, Goodcall
   US$79, Smith.ai US$95; a typical SMB pays US$109-299/month; replaces a
   US$1,500-3,000/month human receptionist. [AgentZap](https://agentzap.ai/blog/ai-receptionist-pricing-complete-cost-guide-2025),
   [Vellum](https://www.vellum.ai/blog/best-ai-receptionist-for-small-business). STRONG.
2. **Missed-call text-back**: 27% of calls to service businesses go
   unanswered; 85% of callers never call back; this recovers 30-60% of
   them; an emergency plumbing ticket runs US$850-1,200. The #1 hook of the
   category for trades. STRONG.
3. **AI-powered WhatsApp selling**: LATAM conversational commerce
   US$18,200M in 2025 (+35%), 72% via WhatsApp; 28-38% conversion with AI.
   [Scala](https://scala-technologies.com/blog/latin-america-ai-sales-automation-2026-whatsapp-commerce-market-opportunity).
   Brazil: 91% of internet users on WhatsApp. STRONG.
4. **Taking orders via chat**: Yalo — ~500,000 corner stores order from
   CPGs over WhatsApp; Coca-Cola FEMSA +30% ticket size, −50% service cost.
   Wati sells catalog+cart starting at US$99/month. STRONG.
5. **Collecting payment in chat**: Pix US$6.7B in 2025, P2B overtook P2P
   (Sep-2025); Magie (Brazil) runs a bank over WhatsApp, 150,000+
   customers. STRONG in Brazil / MEDIUM elsewhere.
6. **Transcribing WhatsApp voice notes**: a top use case for Zapia (1M+
   LATAM users, +45% monthly); **Meta shipped native transcription in the
   Business Calling API (Jun-2026, 54 languages)** ⇒ validated demand, but
   the unit price trends to zero. STRONG.
7. **Transcription + meeting summaries**: Otter US$8-10, Fireflies US$10,
   Fathom US$15-19/seat; a mature, near-commodity category. STRONG.
8. **AI-generated quotes/estimates**: the client expects a quote in <24h;
   doing it by hand takes 2-4h; QuoteIQ generates one from
   photos+description for US$29.99/month; BuildFolio US$39/month; Jobber
   added AI quotes. Speed translates directly into closed sales. STRONG.
9. **AI bookkeeping**: Digits US$69-199/month, Bench US$199/month. Intuit
   2025: top automations = payroll 47%, AP/AR 46%, data entry 43%. a16z:
   the "vertical AI in accounting" thesis (Jan-2025). STRONG.
10. **Marketing/social = the #1 use case by adoption**: Verizon 2025
    (n=600): 28% marketing/social, 24% written communications; PayPal
    2025: content generation ranks #1. STRONG adoption / MEDIUM retention.
11. **A complete graphic piece (image+copy+hashtags)**: Canva Magic Studio
    — 45M business owners; 42% of designs use AI; Predis/Ocoya sell
    "prompt → finished post" for US$15-79/month. STRONG volume, low unit
    price.
12. **FAQ chatbot + order status**: Salesforce SMB 2025 — the most cited
    use cases: FAQs, order inquiries, summaries, recommendations; 30% of
    service cases resolved by AI (projected 50% by 2027). STRONG adoption,
    retention conditional on quality.
13. **General-purpose WhatsApp assistant**: Zapia 1M+ users; "Zapia
    Conecta" contacts businesses on your behalf. MEDIUM (it's free:
    willingness-to-pay unproven).
14. **"AI that acts, not just assists"**: PayPal Jun-2025 (n≈1,000): 82%
    believe adopting it is essential; ~1 in 10 owners are agentic early
    adopters (Intuit). MEDIUM (intent, not a purchase).
15. **Competitor/price monitoring**: Prisync US$99-799/month (Shopify
    plans from US$49); Anthropic's State of AI Agents lists competitor
    monitoring as growing. MEDIUM (a well-paying e-commerce niche).
16. **Agentic browsing**: agentic browsers at 10M MAU (Comet ~3M, Atlas
    ~5M). WEAK as a sellable capability for SMBs today.
17. **Native AI on the platforms where LATAM SMBs actually sell**:
    Tiendanube US$10M→20M+ invested in AI (Chat Nube, Lumi: descriptions,
    SEO, image editing); the first e-commerce platform integrated with
    Meta's Business AI; MELI GenAds. STRONG as validation AND as a
    commoditization threat.
18. **LATAM adoption**: Microsoft 2025 — 54% of SMBs in the Americas use
    AI (49% GenAI); Argentina: >85% started in 2024-25, mostly on basic
    tools. STRONG context.
19. **Conversational banking (Brazil exports it)**: Magie offers credit
    over WhatsApp, expanding to Colombia/Peru/Europe. MEDIUM.
20. **CADE (Brazil, Jan-2026) ordered Meta to allow third-party AI
    chatbots on WhatsApp** — the market is open to non-Meta assistants.
    MEDIUM (structural).
21. **Voice as an interface**: Banco do Brasil's Pix by image/audio via
    WhatsApp (Oct-2025). MEDIUM.
22. **LATAM price anchor**: Cliengo US$24-250/month (real entry point
    ~US$45); a typical AI WhatsApp chatbot US$59-199/month with a basic
    CRM. STRONG as a reference point.

## Capabilities that sound good but DON'T retain

- **Standalone generic copywriting**: Jasper −87% adoption from its peak
  (rank 325→1,168 in spend). [Cledara Q3-2026](https://www.cledara.com/blog/ai-software-spend-winners-losers-q3-2026).
- **Image generation as a separate subscription**: Midjourney −46%
  adoption, −37% spend — image generation went native inside the big
  platforms.
- **Writing correction/assistance**: Grammarly −29% of paying companies
  in 90 days (they cite Claude as the replacement); Perplexity −31%.
- **A chatbot that doesn't resolve anything**: 53-77% of consumers get
  frustrated; a bad bot interaction raises end-customer churn by 67%;
  poorly implemented chatbots = 18% MORE spend on support. [CMR
  Berkeley](https://cmr.berkeley.edu/2026/04/chatbot-frustration-is-real-hidden-costs-and-best-practices/).
- **Mass-produced social content ("sameness"/AI slop)**: AI articles
  outnumbered human ones online in 2025; the 2026 trend swings back to
  real voices. [Hootsuite Social Trends 2026](https://www.hootsuite.com/research/social-trends).
- **"AI" with no concrete verb**: small-business adoption FELL 42%→28%
  (NEXT Apr-2025, n=1,500); 55% cite cost, 62% see no benefit. The gap
  with the 68-75% surveys is "I tried ChatGPT" vs "I pay for something
  that works".
- **Agent washing**: Gartner Jun-2025 — >40% of agentic projects will be
  canceled before the end of 2027; only ~130 real "agentic" vendors out of
  thousands claiming to be.
- **AI apps in general**: subscriptions get canceled ~30% faster than
  non-AI apps; gross retention sits below the ~82% typical of B2B SaaS.
- **Voice agents outside of "missed call = lost money"**: a16z 2025 —
  willingness to pay shows up in high-salary coaching, not in generic
  SMBs.
- **Learning curve**: PayPal 2025 — 74% of SMBs exploring AI won't commit
  without clear ROI (74%) and easier tools (73%).

## Cross-cutting pattern across pricing pages

What gets bought with retention: (1) answering the inbound channel 24/7,
(2) scheduling, (3) quoting fast, (4) collecting payment in chat, (5)
reconciling the books, (6) transcribing, (7) the complete post with its
image. What churns: generic writing, standalone images, a chatbot that
doesn't resolve anything, "AI" with no concrete verb.
