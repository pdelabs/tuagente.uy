# Research 1 — What Uruguayan SMBs do and where AI fits in (8/19/2026)

Research agent, sources verified online. Backbone: the ANDE-INE-Dinapyme
National Survey of Micro/Small Enterprises (collected 2024, published 2025;
1,532 + 1,683 cases) and the UTDT/Fundar/Observatorio PyME/BID AI adoption
survey (Aug-Sep 2025).

## 1. The shape of the SMB landscape and its back-office pain

- **Companies with 1-9 employees ARE the market: 93.3% of formal
  companies.** [ANDE/INE/MIEM-Dinapyme Surveys 2023-24](https://ande.org.uy/images/2025/Informe_de_Resultados-_Encuestas_a_Micro_y_Pequenas_Empresas_2023.pdf)
  — 126,162 micro-businesses (1-4) + 12,410 small ones (5-9). STRONG.
- **Two out of three micro-businesses (68%) are self-employment with no
  employees**, typically personal/professional services; average age 6.6
  years, high turnover. STRONG.
- **Half of micro-businesses operate under simplified tax regimes**:
  Monotributo 21%, Monotributo Social MIDES 10.5%, Literal E 18.7%; only 33%
  under the general regime. It flips for small businesses: 86.2% general
  regime. STRONG.
- **Precarious back-office: only ~50% of micro-businesses track income and
  expenses; 46.3% estimate their profits**; "sufficient bookkeeping": 27.8%
  micro / 66% small; 2.4 out of 7 management practices in place. STRONG.
- **When an SMB pays for advisory services, it's for
  admin/accounting/finance**: only 6.8% micro / 13.1% small businesses hired
  consulting, and of those 65.6% / 59.4% was in admin/accounting/finance; IT
  is second among small businesses (33%). STRONG.
- **Online government paperwork = a top-3 business internet use case**
  (lower outside Montevideo: 53.2% vs 60.3% in Montevideo). STRONG.
- **E-invoicing: CFE mandatory for all VAT taxpayers starting 1/1/2026**;
  benefits for Literal E and <750,000 UI extended to 12/31/2026; risk of
  fines and suspension of the Certificado Único. [J&L Technology](https://jltechnology.com.uy/blog/obligatoriedad-facturacion-electronica-2025),
  Memory/Siigo, YoFacturo. STRONG.
- **Aguinaldo (year-end bonus): a semi-annual obligation the press and
  payroll firms re-explain every cycle** (1st installment 6/30 per Decree
  113/026; calculated from Dec-May pay /12). [El Observador](https://www.elobservador.com.uy/nacional/medio-aguinaldo-2026-la-fecha-limite-el-primer-pago-uruguay-y-como-se-calcula-el-monto-n6043720),
  [Ámbito](https://www.ambito.com/uruguay/hasta-cuando-se-puede-pagar-y-como-se-calcula-el-medio-aguinaldo-n6289315). MEDIUM.
- **Collections: bank transfer dominates (72% micro / 84.2% small)**;
  physical payment networks (Abitab/RedPagos) receive payments for 27.6%
  micro / 39.3% small businesses that sell online. STRONG.
- **Food service: "only 10% of the sector is profitable"**, net
  profitability 4-9%; CAMBADU sells bookkeeping services to its 3,000
  members — the sector outsources its paperwork. [Montevideo Portal](https://www.montevideo.com.uy/Columnistas/Opinion--Del-rubro-gastronomico-solo-el-10-es-rentable-el-resto-empata-o-pierde-uc762621),
  [CAMBADU Oct-2025](https://www.cambadu.com.uy/cambadu-hoy/310.pdf). MEDIUM.
- **Neighborhood retail: independent family-run corner stores = the largest
  number of points of sale** in grocery retail. [Uruguay XXI Retail Sector
  2025](https://www.uruguayxxi.gub.uy/es/centro-informacion/informes/pymes/). STRONG.

## 2. Sales channels: WhatsApp first, a company's own website last

- **WhatsApp/messaging = the #1 online sales channel: 39% micro / 43.5%
  small** (micro outside Montevideo: 46.5%). Social media 2nd (25.5%); a
  company's own website/app only 13-18.3%; marketplaces 12.1-16.5%.
  ANDE/INE Table IX.5. STRONG.
- **48.6% micro / 55.1% small businesses sold online in 2023, but as
  secondary income** (~20-23% of revenue, median 5-8%). Obstacles: product
  not suited to it (36.6%/48.7%), preference for traditional channels
  (32.3%/42.9%), **lack of trained staff (~30%, worse outside
  Montevideo)**. STRONG.
- **Digital presence = IG/MELI/PedidosYa before a company's own website:
  41.7% of micro-businesses use an external website vs 17.2% with their
  own.** Of those with a website, less than half accept orders and less
  than a third take payment online. **The #1 reason for not having a
  website: "no la necesito" (54.6%).** STRONG.
- **Mercado Libre = de facto infrastructure**: the reference point for 72%
  of internet users; ~10,000 SMBs sell on it; 89,000 sellers get paid via
  Mercado Pago (54,000 with QR). [ICEX e-País 2025](https://www.icex.es/content/dam/icex/centros/uruguay/documentos/2025/resumen-ejecutivo-informe-e-pais-comercio-electronico-uruguay-2025.pdf). STRONG.
- **Uruguayan e-commerce: USD 1,563M in 2024 (+38%), >$100,000M in 2025
  (CEDU)**; the reported bottleneck: "bajo nivel de digitalización de las
  pymes". STRONG.
- **Meta: +85% of transactions completed inside WhatsApp in 2025**; Mexico:
  64% of SMB e-commerce goes through WhatsApp (AMVO). MEDIUM (regional).

## 3. AI adoption (2025-2026)

- **48% of Uruguayan adults use AI; 1 in 5 at work, 1 in 3 plans to**;
  occasional, bottom-up usage. [UTDT/Fundar/Obs.PyME/BID, Aug-Sep
  2025](https://repositorio.utdt.edu/items/953af5a2-697a-4978-b389-18d54b2291e3). STRONG.
- **What it's used for at work (UY): information search, data analysis,
  marketing, administrative tasks** — a complement, not a substitute.
  [Búsqueda](https://www.busqueda.com.uy/tecnologia/los-uruguayos-ya-adoptan-la-inteligencia-artificial-pero-entenderla-del-todo-n5405999). STRONG.
- **Barrier #1: lack of knowledge (49%), ahead of cost or access**; only
  16% of companies have a formal strategy, <15% train staff on it; 14.9%
  report high understanding. STRONG.
- **Rioplatense SMBs: adoption is accelerating but concentrated in basic
  tools** (ChatGPT), not processes. [nadia/Obs.PyME Argentina
  2026](https://nadia.ar/extras/nadia_encuesta_pymesARG2026.pdf). MEDIUM
  (proxy).
- **Uruguay: the widest generative-AI reach in South America (22.5% of
  people, Microsoft H2-2025); 3rd in the ILIA-CEPAL index.** MEDIUM
  (context).
- **The State is investing: ANDE+BID US$7M in 2025-26 (~200 projects),
  another US$3M in 2027 for digitalization and AI; "Modo Digital" vouchers
  up to $U 800,000 (60% non-refundable).** [Sarandí](https://www.sarandi690.com.uy/2026/08/07/ande-proyecta-invertir-us-3-millones-en-2027-en-digitalizacion-e-inteligencia-artificial-para-empresas/),
  [ANDE](https://www.ande.org.uy/convocatorias/item/vouchers-de-modo-digital-2.html). STRONG.
- **Tourism: Mintur+UTEC's "AcelerIA"** program trains tourism businesses in
  AI. MEDIUM.

## 4. Local anecdotes

- **Clean-In (cleaning, +1,000 employees): payroll settlements went from 15
  people to 1 + an AI agent** (vendor: Agentico). [La Onda Digital](https://www.laondadigital.uy/automatizacion-con-ia-en-uruguay-que-empresas-ya-lo-estan-haciendo-y-cuanto-ahorran/).
  WEAK-MEDIUM (figures from the vendor).
- **Gorillaz (wholesale bakery): an agent takes orders over WhatsApp 24/7,
  validates against inventory, builds deliveries** — "zero lost orders".
  WEAK-MEDIUM.
- **ROMA AI: 24 SMB clients in 6 months automating multi-channel orders,
  quotes, scheduling, and collections** — a catalog of real pain points;
  typical savings 20-30 hrs/month per employee; local vendors starting at
  US$500 (Ainhoa, Neowyze, Agentify). WEAK.
- **Agriculture: GanaderIA (drone+AI for weighing livestock), Chipsafer,
  +60 agtech startups** at the 2025 demoday. MEDIUM.

## 5. Specific local needs

- **Tenders: 4 local tools exist SOLELY to monitor ARCE** (Tenderis, Gubly,
  ialicitaciones.com, ProveedorUY); abbreviated tenders are published with
  a minimum 8 business days' notice — a short window, needs frequent
  monitoring; open data via [Open Contracting](https://data.open-contracting.org/en/publication/43). MEDIUM (convergent).
- **Certificado Único (DGI/BPS): without it a company gets locked out**
  (banks, the State, imports); a recurring check. WEAK-MEDIUM.
- **Law 20,446 (Dec-2025) changed overseas purchases** (USD 800 duty-free
  allowance from May 2026, with 22% VAT, because of Temu) — retailers track
  these deadlines closely. STRONG (fact) / MEDIUM (as a pain point).
- **The calendar of obligations (aguinaldo, DGI/BPS deadlines, holidays)
  gets republished every year** — the same calculation explained from
  scratch each cycle. MEDIUM.

Gaps in public information: there's no data on the % of government
contracts awarded to micro/small/medium businesses, nor an AI-adoption
survey specific to Uruguayan SMBs.
